Tayho

n. Laois

Any variety of savoury packet snack. Comes from a local pronunciation of the popular brand of Irish potato crisp (chip if yer a yank) called Tayto. If you're from Laois or Offaly, "a packeh of Tayho," could mean Skips, Meanies, Chickatees, Rancheros, Monster / Mighty Munch, Snax, Tayto crisps themselves or any other savoury packet snack for that matter!!!

Posh English

I may accompany you my good man, as I am positively pining for several sherries and a potato crisp sandwich.
