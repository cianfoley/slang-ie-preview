Virgin Megastore

n. Sligo

Nickname for the Mercy Convent. Yungfella1: "Dem youngwans in de Virgin Megastore are a pack of pure dry, moany, wreck de heads." Yungfella2, in agreement, mutters: "Ahoii!"

Posh English

Attending that religious seminary has never been more straightforward now the entrance requirements have decreased for the programmes there.
