Ask me arse

exp. Dublin

An impolite way of saying no to someone whilst instructing them to feck off. "Go and shyte" is often used as an alternative.

Posh English

Inform Anthony that if my culinary sandwiches do not meet his exacting standards, he can kindly swivel!
