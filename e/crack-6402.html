Crack

n. All Ireland

Slang word for fun, involving alcohol and music. Craic, the gaelicized version is often labelled as faux-Irish as it is not Irish per se. The term is said to originate from Scotland and has been adopted throughout the whole of Ireland without exception. Often used in a greeting as in
