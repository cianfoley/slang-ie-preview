Gowl

n. Waterford

A girls private parts. Also: Vagina, also used as a verb to get up to mischief, to joke, mess, take the piss in a friendly way. Also: Female genitalia.

Posh English

I will give her a kick in the vagina in a moment if she does not shut up.
