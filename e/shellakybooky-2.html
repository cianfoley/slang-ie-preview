Shellakybooky

n. Waterford

Any of numerous aquatic (Perry Winkle) or terrestrial (Snail) molluscs, typically having a spirally coiled shell. A Snail.

Posh English

Little mollusc of the garden, extend your sensory tentacles and observe the aristocratic lady approaching for a visit.
